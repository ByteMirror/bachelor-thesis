### Issue 1: Compound adjective hyphenation (Line 596)
- **Original**: `ist es performance-kritisch`

- **Reason**: German compound adjectives are typically written as one word without hyphen. The pattern follows "sicherheitskritisch", "zeitkritisch", etc.

### Issue 2: Missing Durchkopplung in compound with dimensions (Line 689)
- **Original**: `4096×4096 Pixel Map mit 64 Pixel Grid-Größe`
- **Correction**: `4096$\times$4096-Pixel-Map mit 64-Pixel-Grid-Größe`
- **Reason**: Durchkopplung requires hyphenation in multi-part compounds. When dimensions modify a noun, they should be connected with hyphens. Also changed `×` to `$\times$` for proper LaTeX math rendering.
