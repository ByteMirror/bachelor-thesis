## Detailed Issue Analysis

### Issue 1: Line 236 - Durchkopplung
**Original**: `service-orientierte Architektur`
**Corrected**: `Service-orientierte Architektur`
**Rule**: In compound adjectives derived from English nouns used as attributive adjectives before a German noun, the first component (the English noun "Service") should be capitalized to maintain consistency with German noun capitalization rules for compound words.

### Issue 2: Line 262 - Durchkopplung
**Original**: `\ac{GM}-only Elemente`
**Corrected**: `\ac{GM}-only-Elemente`
**Rule**: When an English multi-word phrase (here "GM-only") is used attributively before a German noun, the entire compound must be hyphenated according to German Durchkopplung rules.

### Issue 3: Line 317 - Durchkopplung
**Original**: `Standard Plugin Lifecycle Hooks`
**Corrected**: `Standard-Plugin-Lifecycle-Hooks`
**Rule**: Multi-word English terms used as a compound German noun require complete hyphenation (Durchkopplung). Each word in the compound must be connected with hyphens.

### Issue 4: Line 333 - Gender Agreement
**Original**: `Diese Constraint`
**Corrected**: `Dieses Constraint`
**Rule**: The English loanword "Constraint" is grammatically neuter (das Constraint) in German. The demonstrative pronoun must agree in gender, thus "dieses" (neuter) instead of "diese" (feminine).

### Issue 5: Line 337 - Durchkopplung (Two Instances)
**Original**: `PIXI.js v7 Bundle`
**Corrected**: `PIXI.js-v7-Bundle`
**Rule**: Version numbers within compound nouns require hyphenation.

**Rule**: Two-word English technical terms used as German nouns require hyphenation.
