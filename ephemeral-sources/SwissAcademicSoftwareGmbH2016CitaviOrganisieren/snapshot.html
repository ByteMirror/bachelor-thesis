Citavi - The Only All-in-One Writing and Referencing Solution

"From chaos to clarity - Your Knowledge Management Center"

Product Description

Citavi functions as an integrated reference management tool emphasizing knowledge organization. The platform enables researchers to "tackle the increasing complexity of research writing in today's information landscape."

Core Features

Organization & Management

  • Knowledge Organizer for structuring information
  • Document categorization and tagging capabilities
  • Quick information retrieval across sources

Research Writing Acceleration

  • Automatic outline generation in Microsoft Word with quotes and summaries
  • Support for over 11,000 citation styles (APA, Chicago, MLA, Turabian, etc.)
  • Custom citation style creation options

AI-Powered Capabilities

  • Literature discovery claimed 4x faster
  • Text summarization and complex term definitions
  • Key findings extraction from articles

Collaboration Features

  • Real-time colleague communication
  • Task assignment and tracking
  • Cloud-based or server-based deployment options
  • Secure document sharing and export

Product Editions

Citavi Web: Browser-based for cross-platform access
Citavi Windows: Desktop software for Windows users
DBServer: Enterprise data-secure server installation
Enterprise Licensing: Customized packages available

Additional Support

Free trial access and institutional site licenses offered through universities and research organizations.
